Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SnipAlert collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our salon discovery and booking platform.

1. Information we collect

We collect the information you give us when creating an account: name, username, mobile number, email address, account role, and password. Passwords are stored in protected form, not as readable text.

For salon owners, we collect salon name, owner details, opening hours, location, salon and service photos, service details, and descriptions. When you make or manage a booking, we record the selected salon, service, booking status, and feedback. We use your device location only when you choose to share it to find nearby salons. If you enable alerts, we also process the notification subscription needed to send booking updates.

2. How we use your information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, verify email addresses with OTPs, show salons and services, process bookings, display feedback, send booking or account messages, provide nearby-salon results, and maintain the safety and reliability of SnipAlert.

3. When we share information

We share the limited information needed to complete a booking with the relevant salon owner, such as your username, selected service, and booking status. Public salon listings and feedback may be visible to other users. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users, SnipAlert, or the public from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

4. Your choices and rights

You may update your profile information through your account. You can decline location or notification permissions in your browser or device settings. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. A salon owner can remove a salon through the OTP-protected removal flow; this hides the salon from customers while retaining the record for operational and legal purposes.

5. Data retention and security

We keep information only for as long as needed to operate SnipAlert, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and maintain legitimate records. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Children and policy updates

SnipAlert is not intended for children who cannot legally consent to use the service. If we make material changes to this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.

7. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email supportsalon@gmail.com.
