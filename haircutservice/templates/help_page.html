Account setup

Create the right SnipAlert account

Click Signup, complete your details, then select the role that fits you in the final dropdown.

General customer

  1. Click the Signup button.
  2. Enter your name, username, mobile number, email, and password.
  3. Select Customer in the dropdown.
  4. Submit the form and verify the OTP sent to your email.

After verification, use Male Services or Female Services to book a haircut, facial, beard trim, or other service.

{% if not user.is_authenticated %}Create Customer Account{% endif %}

Shopkeeper

  1. Click the Signup button and fill in your details.
  2. Select Shopkeeper in the dropdown.
  3. Submit the form and verify the OTP from your email.
  4. Open your profile and select Register Salon.

Add your salon details, location, opening hours, and services to start receiving bookings.
